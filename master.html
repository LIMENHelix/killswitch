Step 1 · Show the owner what we built
Point it at a page we have already built. It reads the business name off the page, puts it live at its own address, and emails the owner their panel link. Google cannot see it yet: the page carries noindex until you press Step 2, so if they go quiet, nothing of theirs was ever public. Nothing is retyped and nothing is duplicated.
Step 2 · They said yes
Only press this once the owner has actually accepted. It removes the noindex, which is the point of no return: from here Google can find and keep their page. Everything before this is invisible to search.
