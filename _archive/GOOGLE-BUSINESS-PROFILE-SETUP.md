# KILLSWITCH — Google Business Profile (free local leads)

When someone in KC searches "web designer near me" or "kansas city website,"
Google shows a 3-business map pack. This is how you get in it. It's free, it
works while you sleep, and **reviews are the #1 lever.**

---

## PART 1 — Create the profile (~15 min)

1. Go to **business.google.com** → sign in with the Google account that should own this
   (a business Gmail if you have one; otherwise your main account).
2. Search **Killswitch** → "Create a business with this name."
3. **Primary category:** **Website designer.**
4. **"Add a location customers can visit?"** → **No.** (You don't want clients at your house.)
5. **Service area:** add **Kansas City, MO** + the metro you'll serve (Overland Park,
   Olathe, Lee's Summit, Independence, Kansas City KS). This is what you rank for.
6. **Phone** + **Website** → `killswitch.domains`.
7. Finish → it sends you to verification (Part 2).

**Business name = just "Killswitch."** Do NOT stuff it ("Killswitch Web Design Kansas City")
— Google suspends profiles for that. The *category* does that job, not the name.

---

## PART 2 — Verify (the gate; runs in the background)

Google confirms you're real before the profile shows publicly.
- You'll be offered **phone/text, email, video, or postcard** — pick the fastest (usually phone or video).
- **Video verification** (common for service businesses): a short clip showing proof you're
  legit — your work setup, logging into killswitch.domains / your Stripe. Just follow the prompts.
- Phone = minutes. Video/postcard = a few days. Until verified, you won't show in search — so start this now and keep going on Part 3 while it processes.

---

## PART 3 — Fill it out 100% (complete profiles rank higher)

1. **Categories:** keep "Website designer" primary; **add** "Software company,"
   "Internet marketing service," "Marketing agency."
2. **Description:** paste the one below.
3. **Services:** add each (name + short blurb + price): Launch Page $149, Starter $499,
   Business Website $2,500, Custom Software, Care Plan $99/mo — plus AI chat, online booking,
   payments/e-commerce, dashboards & CRM. (Mirror your pricing page.)
4. **Hours:** set real hours.
5. **Booking link:** GBP has an "Appointments / Bookings" link field → paste your **Calendly**
   (`https://calendly.com/chrishubbel72/30min`).
6. **Photos** (huge for clicks): logo, a cover image, and 4–5 screenshots of real work —
   LIMEN Helix, AllAccessKC, Recursive Love, and the Killswitch site itself.
7. Turn on **Messaging** if you want texts straight from the listing.

**Description (paste-ready, under 750 chars):**
```
KILLSWITCH builds custom websites and business software for Kansas City — fast, modern sites plus the systems behind them: online booking, payments, dashboards, CRMs, and AI assistants. Whether you need a sharp one-page site or a full custom platform, you work directly with the person who builds it — no agency runaround, no offshore handoffs, no jargon. Honest, fixed pricing starting at $149, most sites live within a week, and you own everything. Serving Kansas City and the metro. If you can describe it, we can build it.
```

---

## PART 4 — The thing that actually wins: REVIEWS

Reviews are the #1 factor for ranking in the map pack AND getting clicked. A profile with
5 reviews beats a perfect profile with zero.

- The second you finish ANY build — even a free/test one for a friend — ask for a Google review.
- **Get your review link:** GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews" → copy the short link.
- Text it: *"Mind leaving a quick Google review? Takes 30 seconds — here: [link]"*
- **Goal: 5 reviews fast.** Friends/family whose sites you've touched count. Just get to 5.

---

## PART 5 — Keep it alive (5 min/week)

- Post a weekly **Update** (GBP Posts): something you shipped, an offer ("$149 one-page
  sites this month"), or a quick tip. Active profiles rank higher.
- Reply to **every review** and any **Q&A**.

---

## Do this now (in order)
1. Create + category + service area (Part 1).
2. Start verification (Part 2) — let it run.
3. Description, services, Calendly link, photos (Part 3).
4. Text 3 people who've seen your work for a review (Part 4).

This is your free lead engine. It compounds: the more complete + reviewed, the higher you
rank, the more "web designer near me" calls land in your phone.
