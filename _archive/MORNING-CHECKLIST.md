## STEP 1 — Find 10 leads (~15 min)
Google Maps → search a KC category, click listings, grab the ones with a bad/no website.
- 🎯 No website at all · 🎯 a `wixsite.com`/`squarespace.com`/`godaddysites.com` site · 🎯 old/ugly/not-mobile
- Log each in the sheet: **Business · Area · Trade · Site problem · Email (if you can find it) · Phone**
- Rotate categories so you never run dry: plumbers, roofers, HVAC, electricians, salons, barbers, nails, med-spas, cafés, food trucks, caterers, landscapers, auto, cleaning, fitness, painters.
- *(Email tip: check their site footer / "Contact" page, or the Google listing. No email? Use the phone for a quick call instead.)*

## STEP 2 — Draft each with the Switch brain (~10 min)
For each lead: open the Switch brain, drop in name/area/trade/site/email → it writes the angle + 3 emails.
- Hit **📨 Compose email** on **Day 1** → it opens in Proton, pre-filled → **read it once**, tweak if needed, **Send.**
- Mark the sheet: **Status = Sent · Date.**

## STEP 3 — Send today's follow-ups (~10 min)
Check the sheet for anyone you emailed **3 days ago** and **6 days ago** who hasn't replied:
- **Day 3** → send Email 2 (the free-mockup nudge)
- **Day 6** → send Email 3 (the soft last touch + Calendly link)
- No reply after Day 6 → mark **Cold**, move on. (You can re-try them in a couple months.)

## STEP 4 — Handle replies (do these FIRST, before new sends)
- **Interested?** → Offer the **free mockup**: spend ~30 min building a real one-page version of THEIR site, send a screenshot/link → *"Want it live? $149."* This converts like nothing else.
- **Wants to talk?** → Send the **Calendly link** or just call them.
- **Closed a build?** → Always pitch the **$99/mo Care Plan**. The build pays once; the Care Plan pays every month — that's the real business.
