# KILLSWITCH — The Wix-Refugee Lead Play

People stuck with an abandoned/ugly Wix or Squarespace site — or NO site at all —
are your warmest market. They already know they need it, they've already failed at
DIY, and you're the "done-for-you, real human, live today" answer. Lead with the
**$149 Launch Page ("live in 30 minutes").**

---

## WHO TO HIT (priority order)
1. **No website at all** (Google listing, no link) → easiest yes. They know it's a problem.
2. **Free Wix/Squarespace subdomain** (`xxx.wixsite.com`, `xxx.squarespace.com`, `godaddysites.com`, `weebly.com`) → DIY refugee, stuck, never even got a real domain.
3. **Ugly / broken / not-mobile site** → they're embarrassed by it.

## HOW TO FIND THEM (~30 min/day — the "Google Maps sweep")
1. Search Google Maps for a KC category: *"plumbers Kansas City," "hair salons Overland Park," "coffee shops KCMO," "roofers Olathe," "med spa Lee's Summit."*
2. Click each listing → look at **Website**:
   - No website → 🎯 hot
   - `wixsite.com` / `squarespace.com` / `godaddysites.com` → 🎯 refugee
   - old/ugly/not-mobile → 🎯
3. Log it in a sheet: **Business | Phone | What's wrong | Channel | Status**
4. Rotate categories: home services (plumber, HVAC, electrician, roofer, landscaper, painter), beauty (salon, barber, nails, med-spa, lashes), food (cafe, food truck, caterer, BBQ), trades, fitness, auto, cleaning.

**Facebook:** join KC small-biz groups (KC Small Business Network, KC Entrepreneurs, neighborhood + Chamber groups). Watch for "anyone know a web person?" and reply. Scan business pages with no/bad site link.

## THE SECRET WEAPON: the free mockup
You can build a one-page site in ~30 minutes. For any **warm** prospect (they replied / showed interest), build a rough version of **their** site and send a screenshot or link:
> "Here's what yours could look like — want it live? $149."

Seeing it *real* converts like nothing else. Don't do this for everyone (it's your time) — only the ones who bite.

## WHY YOU, NOT WIX (the pitch)
- **Done FOR you** — they already failed at DIY.
- **Live this week** — often same day.
- **A real, live human** (you), not a chatbot or template.
- **You own everything.**
- **From $149** — cheaper than the 20 hours they'd burn fighting Wix.

## DAILY RHYTHM (keep it dead simple)
- 30 min: Maps sweep → log 10–15 prospects.
- 30 min: reach out to ~10 (call / DM / email).
- Reply in 1–2 KC Facebook groups.
- Send a free mockup to anyone warm.
- **Goal: ~4–6 new clients/month = your target.**
