# Killswitch AI Sales Rep — Product Package

## One-liner
**An AI sales rep that fills your calendar with qualified meetings — on autopilot.
Done for you by Killswitch.**

## Name (pick one)

## What it does (HONEST scope — sell exactly this)
Finds your ideal customers → writes a personal pitch to each → sends and follows up
relentlessly → qualifies the replies → **books meetings straight onto your calendar.**
Works nights, weekends, holidays. Never quits, never has a bad day, costs a fraction of a hire.

**It books the meetings. You (or your closer) close them.** Say this out loud — a
"meeting machine," not a magic closer. That honesty is what keeps you refund-free.

## Who it's for
Local + B2B businesses that need more leads and can't afford / can't find / don't want
to manage a salesperson: contractors, agencies, consultants, B2B services, med-spas, etc.

## Delivery model
**Done-for-you service** (you in the loop, AI does the work). Sellable today, honest.
Self-serve SaaS dashboard comes later, once it's dialed. Don't sell the SaaS yet.

## Pricing (recommended)
**Standard**
- **Setup:** $500 one-time (sending domain, warmup, list build, copy, launch)
- **$1,000/mo** done-for-you — the engine runs, qualified meetings land on your calendar

**Founding-client offer** (first 5 clients — get case studies fast)
- **$250 setup + $500/mo**, locked 6 months, in exchange for a testimonial
- Honest framing: *"I'm taking 5 founding clients at half price while I build the case studies."*

**Trust booster (optional):** *"A booked meeting in 30 days or next month's free."*
> ⚠ Don't promise a specific meeting *count* you can't guarantee — promise the engine + relentless tuning until it lands.

## Objections → answers
- **"Is this spam?"** → No. Personalized, B2B, compliant, opt-outs honored. Quality, not blast.
- **"Will it replace my salesperson?"** → It does the grunt work — finding, outreach, follow-up, booking — so your closer only talks to warm, qualified people.
- **"What if it doesn't work?"** → Founding guarantee + we keep tuning the targeting and copy until it does.
- **"Why not just hire an SDR?"** → An SDR is $4–6k/mo + benefits + 3-month ramp + turnover. This is a fraction, no management, no HR, scales the day you need it.

**V1 done-for-you (now)** → V2 semi-self-serve dashboard → V3 SaaS. Sell the service, build toward the product. Prove every claim on Killswitch first.
