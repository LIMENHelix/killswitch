# KILLSWITCH — Delivery-Call Scripts (KC top 6)

These are **delivery** calls, not cold pitches. The site already exists, so you are
handing over something real, not asking permission to build. That flips the whole call:
you are not selling, you are delivering, and mentioning the paid part is just honesty.

Internal reference. Lives in `_outreach/` (firewalled from deploy). Voice matches
`_playbook/outbound-playbook-v1.md` — disarm first, be specific, one-word next step.

## BEFORE YOU DIAL (must be true or the close breaks)
- [ ] The killswitch site is **deployed** so each demo link below is LIVE. Test the exact
      URL on your phone first. If it 404s, the site has not been redeployed since the demos
      were added — deploy, then call. (As of this writing the demos are built but NOT yet live.)
- [ ] You can send a text during/right after the call (the close is "can I text you the link").
- [ ] Call during their business hours, on your lunch or after 5. One call per shop.
- [ ] After each call, log it in the admin dashboard (killswitchwebsites.com/admin).

## THE SHAPE (every call, 30 seconds)
1. **Disarm:** "This is a sales call, you can hang up, no hard feelings. Thirty seconds?"
2. **Their specific hook:** the one true thing about *their* shop.
3. **Deliver:** "I already built you a website. It's real, it's yours, free."
4. **The catch, before they ask:** add-ons $19/mo each, optional, off unless they turn them on.
5. **Zero-risk close:** "Can I text you the link right now?"
